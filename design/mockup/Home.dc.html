Our story

“Tera,” from terra: the earth.

After reactions to synthetic products, Harrison, a materials science engineer, set out to care for his skin with the very earth he trekked across. He and a team of scientists analyzed clays across Europe and the Americas.

Off the coast of the Netherlands they found a naturally occurring magnesium mineral that draws moisture from the air and helps skin hold on to it.
