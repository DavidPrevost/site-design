# Product photography

The new packaging has not been photographed yet. The theme ships with illustrated stand-ins
(`design/placeholders/*.png`) that you can upload as temporary product images (see
[launch-checklist.md](launch-checklist.md)). Replace them once real photos exist.

## How the theme shows photos

- **Product cards:**
  - 4:5 portrait frames by default (**Theme settings → Product cards**).
  - The whole product is shown, centered on a soft cream background.
- **Product page gallery:**
  - Square frames.
  - Thumbnails below on desktop, swipe with dots on phones.
- **White backgrounds melt away.** The theme blends white photo backgrounds into the cream card color, so a
  bottle shot on plain white looks cut out without any editing. Transparent PNG cut-outs work the same way.
- The **first image** of each product is the one used on cards and in search.

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | At least **2000 × 2000 px** (square) or **2000 × 2500 px** (4:5). Shopify makes the smaller sizes. |
| Format | JPG for photos; PNG for cut-outs with a transparent background |
| Background | Plain white or a transparent cut-out for pack shots. Warm neutrals (cream, sand, linen) for texture and lifestyle shots. |
| Framing | Product centered, about 70% of the frame height, with even space around it. Keep every pack shot at the same scale and angle so the three products line up on the collection page. |
| Light | Soft and even, slightly warm. Avoid hard reflections on the pump heads and the amber bottle. |
| Alt text | Describe each image in Shopify (click the image → *Add alt text*), e.g. "Advanced Hydration Facial Cream in a tan airless pump bottle". |

## Shot list (per product)

1. **Front pack shot:** the bottle straight on, label readable. This is the main image.
2. **Back or label detail:** the ingredient panel, legible.
3. **Pump detail:** a close-up of the airless pump (creams) or the black pump head (face wash).
4. **Texture swatch:** a swirl of cream, or lather for the face wash, on a warm neutral surface. The gallery's second
   image in the design is a swatch like this.
5. **In hand:** shows the real size of the bottle.
6. **Lifestyle (optional):** outdoors, on rock or clay, or by a sink, in the palette of the site.

Also useful: **a group shot of all three bottles**. It works as the hero's featured image or for social sharing.
